Driver view (demo)

The driver's phone app. Everything runs in this browser and is saved on this device only. Sample data, nothing is sent.

  1. My Load: tap Loaded → En Route → Delivered. The Load Board and Calendar move with it.
  2. Snap BOL: take or pick a photo. The pay clock, invoice and shipper notice tick in.
  3. Pay: delivered + BOL = paid the same day.
  4. Inbox: the assistant read a rate con. Confirm it to add the load to the board.
  5. Stops: tap a stop for GPS arrival/departure, the free-time bar and Bill detention.
  6. Messages: demo chats; status taps post here.

All companies, numbers and dates are made up. Sample dates are set relative to today so the clocks always show a mix of open and overdue items. See the README for the data model.
